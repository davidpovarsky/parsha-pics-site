Frequently asked questions

Practical details about using, adapting and licensing the resource.

What ages is Parsha Pics suitable for?+−

Parsha Pics is designed primarily for Years 3–6 / KS2, but the same picture sequences can also be used with younger or older pupils by adapting the activity and level of discussion.

What are the illustrations based on?+−

The illustrations are based primarily on Peshat and Rashi, with scenes carefully researched and reviewed with Kodesh educators.

How many pictures are there per Parsha?+−

Each Parsha is divided into its key events. The number of illustrations varies with the story; Lech Lecha is shown here as a nine-picture sequence.

Can we adapt the worksheets?+−

Yes. The Canva templates are editable, and the individual high-resolution pictures can be used in your own teaching materials within your licence.

Can we purchase only one Sefer?+−

Yes. You can request pricing for one Sefer, several Sefarim or the complete collection.

What can our school use the illustrations for?+−

The picture sequences can support classroom presentation, review, sequencing, discussion and worksheets. Specific permitted uses depend on your school licence.
