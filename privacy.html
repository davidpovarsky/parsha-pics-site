Who we are

Parsha Pics by Goldy Povarsky provides illustrated Parsha teaching resources and school licences. Parsha Pics is the controller of personal information collected through this website. You can contact us about privacy at goldy@goldypov.com.

Information we collect

  • the name, email address, school or organisation, selected Sefarim and message that you submit;
  • our correspondence with you and records needed to provide a sample, prepare a quote, fulfil an order or administer a school licence; and
  • limited technical and security information, such as IP address, request time and anti-abuse verification results. We do not receive the secret Turnstile verification key.

The forms are intended for teachers, school staff and other adults. Please do not submit personal information about pupils or other children.

How and why we use it

We use the information to answer enquiries, deliver the requested Lech Lecha evaluation sample for immediate download, provide pricing, take steps at your request before entering a contract, administer orders and licences, keep required business records, and protect the service from abuse. Depending on the context, our lawful basis is taking steps towards or performing a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in responding to genuine enquiries and securing the website. We do not add form submitters to a marketing list without a separate lawful basis.

Who receives the information

We use service providers only as needed to operate the site and respond: Cloudflare hosts the website, provides Turnstile security checks and delivers secure sample downloads; Resend routes form notification emails; and Zoho provides the recipient mailbox. Those providers process information under their own security and data-protection arrangements. We may also disclose information where legally required or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where data-protection law requires it, we rely on the provider’s recognised transfer mechanism and related safeguards. Contact us if you would like more information about the safeguards relevant to your request.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only while handling them and for a limited period afterwards where reasonably needed for follow-up, security or dispute records. If an enquiry becomes an order or licence, we retain the related business and transaction records for the period required by tax, accounting and legal obligations. We then delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your information, object to particular processing, and receive portable information. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier processing. To exercise a right, email goldy@goldypov.com. We may need to verify your identity.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first.

Changes

We may update this notice when the service or providers change. The date above identifies the current version.
